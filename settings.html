School settings

Only Super Admin and School Admin can change these settings. Every change is recorded in the Audit logs.

School details

Timings and working days

Working days

Holidays and vacations in the School calendar are always off. A "Special school day" in the calendar makes a weekly off day a working day.

Rules

Flexibility

Leave empty to let teachers correct attendance of any past date. The office (admin, principal) can always correct it. Holidays and special school days for a single division are set in the School calendar ("Applies to").

Show to parents and students

Untick a section to hide it from the parent and student portal. You can switch it back on any time. Nothing is deleted.

School location on the map

In Google Maps right-click the school gate and click the two numbers shown on top to copy them (for example 17.701212, 74.174362). Put the first number in Latitude and the second in Longitude. They are shown as a map on the Contact page. Leave both empty to hide the map.

Report card wording

Fee payment by UPI

Parents see this only after login, on the Fees tab. Leave empty to switch UPI payment off.

Fee payment by bank transfer (NEFT / IMPS)

Shown to parents only after login, on the Fees tab, with a Copy button. Leave empty to hide. Double-check the account number: parents will send real money to it.

Grading rules (A+, A, B...) are managed on the Exams page. Admission and GR numbers are typed by the office when a student is admitted.
